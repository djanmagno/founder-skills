# E02: Problem validated through manual/concierge delivery

**Complement mode:** none

**Expected skills:** `founder-validate` (confirming the tier) → `founder-mvp`

**Fictional setup:** "Tomas" has been manually reconciling invoices for 6 logistics companies himself, by hand, for a flat monthly fee — no software. 4 of the 6 have paid for 3+ consecutive months. He's exhausted doing it by hand and wants to "finally build the real product."

## Founder's opening message

> I've been doing invoice reconciliation by hand for 6 trucking companies as a paid service — $400/month each, no software, just me in spreadsheets. 4 of them have paid me 3 months in a row now. I'm burned out doing this manually and I think it's proof I should build the real SaaS product now. Where do I start on the build?

## Must not

Recommend rebuilding the product from scratch as the first move, or treat "I'm burned out" as the reason to build rather than the evidence (4 of 6 paying 3+ months = commitment/recurring-use-tier evidence) as the reason. Per `docs/PLAN.md` §11 E02, the failure mode is recommending a rebuild before scoping the smallest useful automation of what's already validated.

## Pass criteria

- Recognizes the situation as "problem validated through manual/concierge delivery" and treats the 4-of-6 recurring payment as strong evidence (payment + recurring use tiers) — doesn't ask Tomas to re-validate from zero.
- Routes to `founder-mvp` for the **smallest** automation of the already-proven manual process, not a full rebuild — explicitly scopes down from "the real SaaS product" to what the evidence actually requires next.
- Names what stays manual for now vs. what gets automated first, tied to what's costing Tomas the most burnout time (not a generic feature list).
- Does not lose the 2 non-paying accounts' data — notes them as evidence gaps (why didn't they convert?) rather than ignoring them.
