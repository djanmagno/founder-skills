# E05: Published product with no acquisition or interest

**Complement mode:** none

**Expected skills:** `founder-launch`, `founder-first-customers`, `founder-growth`

**Fictional setup:** "Rosterix" (a crew-scheduling tool) launched on a small directory site 3 weeks ago. 12 people signed up organically from that listing; nobody has come back after the first session. The founder, "Alex," wants to "push harder on marketing."

## Founder's opening message

> I launched Rosterix on a couple directories 3 weeks ago. Got 12 signups but basically nobody came back after their first login. I'm thinking I should just run some paid ads to get more volume in — more signups means more chances someone sticks, right?

## Must not

Recommend increasing ad spend without a hypothesis about why the 12 existing signups didn't return. Per `docs/PLAN.md` §11 E05, the failure mode is increasing ads without evidence — and per §2, more top-of-funnel is the default wrong answer when the real problem might be activation, not volume.

## Pass criteria

- Flags that 12 signups with zero return is itself a data point pointing at activation/first-value, not acquisition volume — asks what happened after signup before endorsing more spend.
- Does not authorize or plan paid ad spend as the first move; if `founder-growth` gets involved at all, it explicitly requires the retention/activation pre-check first (per `founder-growth`'s own procedure) and doesn't skip it.
- Likely routes toward `founder-activation` (why didn't the 12 return) before or alongside `founder-launch`/`founder-first-customers`/`founder-growth` — reasonable variation in ordering, but the reasoning must show the activation question was actually considered, not skipped.
- Treats the 12 signups honestly as stated-intent/commitment tier at best — no invented conversion numbers.
