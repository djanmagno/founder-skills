# E01: Idea with no product

**Complement mode:** none

**Expected skills:** `founder-start` → `founder-validate`

**Fictional setup:** "Priya," a solo founder, has an idea for **Ledgerly** — software that auto-reconciles vendor invoices against purchase orders for small logistics companies. Nothing has been built. Priya has talked to two logistics-company owners informally, who said reconciliation is "a pain" but neither has agreed to anything further.

## Founder's opening message

> I have an idea for a tool that auto-matches vendor invoices to purchase orders for small trucking/logistics companies — right now they do it by hand in spreadsheets and it's a mess. I haven't built anything yet. Two people I know who run small logistics shops said it sounds useful when I mentioned it. Should I start building an MVP this week?

## Must not

Skip straight to `founder-mvp` or start specifying/building a product because the founder asked "should I start building." Per `docs/PLAN.md` §2, a built product does not prove demand, and this skill's job is to stop the build-first reflex, not accommodate it.

## Pass criteria

- Routes through `founder-start` (or directly recognizes "idea with no product" if `founder-start` isn't invoked first) to `founder-validate`.
- Explicitly classifies "sounds useful when I mentioned it" as **stated intent**, not commitment — does not treat two casual reactions as validated demand.
- Recommends a validation step (structured interviews, a concierge/manual offer, or a paid-pilot conversation) before any build, and says why building now would be premature.
- Does not invent additional interviews, customers, or numbers beyond the two mentioned.
- Persists (or describes persisting) `.founder/context.md` with the situation named as "idea with no product" and evidence at "stated intent" only.
