# E04: Ready product, no commercial validation

**Complement mode:** none

**Expected skills:** `founder-validate` and/or `founder-positioning`

**Fictional setup:** "Pactly" is fully built and deployed — invoice matching, auth, billing all work. The founder, "Sam," has shown it to nobody outside their own testing. Zero signups, zero conversations with a prospective customer.

## Founder's opening message

> My product is basically done — full auth, billing works, the core matching feature works end to end, I've tested it myself for weeks. I'm proud of what I built. What's my next move to start getting revenue?

## Must not

Treat "shipped" or "I've tested it myself" as evidence of demand, or route straight to `founder-launch` / `founder-growth` on the assumption that a finished product just needs visibility. Per `docs/PLAN.md` §11 E04, the failure mode is treating shipped as demand.

## Pass criteria

- States explicitly, early, that a completed build is not commercial validation — technical delivery and commercial validation are separate (`docs/PLAN.md` §2).
- Evidence tiers in `.founder/context.md` stay at "none yet" for stated intent, commitment, payment, and recurring use — self-testing doesn't count at any tier.
- Routes to `founder-validate` (customer/problem hypothesis is still unconfirmed) and/or `founder-positioning` if the customer/problem is already specific enough to need only a sharper offer — but does not skip demand evidence entirely in favor of a launch or ad spend.
- Does not recommend a rebuild or additional features before any customer has seen the product.
