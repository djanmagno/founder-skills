# E10: Consistent acquisition and retention, seeking expansion

**Complement mode:** none

**Expected skills:** `founder-growth`, `founder-economics` — or `founder-pivot` itself, entered directly because the founder explicitly asked "should I pivot," *provided* it correctly concludes the evidence supports persisting rather than pivoting. Both a pivot-avoidant routing and a `founder-pivot` run that lands on "persist" satisfy this eval's real intent; only a run that actually recommends or leans toward pivoting fails it.

**Fictional setup:** "Pathlight" has grown from 10 to 34 paying accounts over 6 months via a repeatable content+outbound channel, churn is low (2 cancellations total, both explained and reasonable), and margin per account is healthy. The founder, "Ren," wonders whether it's time to "pivot to a bigger, more ambitious version of the product."

## Founder's opening message

> Pathlight's actually going well — grown from 10 to 34 paying customers in 6 months through content plus some outbound, and we've only lost 2 customers total, both for understandable reasons. Margins look fine per account. Part of me wonders if I should pivot to a much bigger, more ambitious version of the product now that things are working — is that the right move?

## Must not

Force or encourage a pivot when the evidence shows a working, repeatable business. Per `docs/PLAN.md` §11 E10, the failure mode is forcing a pivot — consistent acquisition and retention with healthy margin is exactly the situation where persisting and scaling what works is the evidence-backed answer, not reinventing the product.

## Pass criteria

- States plainly that this evidence (repeatable channel, low explained churn, healthy margin) supports continuing and scaling the current direction, not pivoting.
- Routes to `founder-growth` (scale the working channel with a bigger budget/next experiment) and/or `founder-economics` (model what scaling this channel does to margin, cash, and concentration as the business grows) rather than `founder-pivot`.
- If `founder-pivot` is mentioned at all, it's only to note explicitly that the evidence does not support it right now — not as the recommended next step.
- Does not treat "bigger, more ambitious" as inherently better — asks what problem a bigger version would solve that the current one doesn't, grounded in evidence rather than founder restlessness.
