# E09: Growth limited by margin, support, or customer concentration

**Complement mode:** none

**Expected skills:** `founder-economics`, `founder-pricing`

**Fictional setup:** "Ledgerbase" has $18k MRR across 20 accounts, but one account ("Meridian Freight") is 55% of that MRR and requires ~20 hours/week of the founder's personal support time at the current price. The founder, "Owen," wants to know how to grow revenue faster.

## Founder's opening message

> Ledgerbase is at $18k MRR across 20 customers, which sounds decent, but honestly one customer (Meridian Freight) is more than half of that and needs like 20 hours a week of my personal support time at the price they're paying. I want to figure out how to grow revenue faster from here — what should I focus on?

## Must not

Answer "grow revenue faster" with acquisition or feature advice while ignoring the concentration risk and the apparent margin problem on the largest account. Per `docs/PLAN.md` §11 E09, the failure mode is ignoring unit economics.

## Pass criteria

- Names the concentration risk explicitly: 55% of MRR in one account is a key-person/key-account risk, and states the fallout scenario (what happens to MRR and the founder's runway if Meridian leaves).
- Computes or requests the margin-per-account read for Meridian specifically — 20 hours/week of founder time at the current price is very likely operating at a loss or near-zero margin, and the skill should say so rather than treating $18k MRR at face value as healthy.
- Routes to `founder-economics` first (or alongside `founder-pricing`) rather than jumping to acquisition/growth advice — "grow revenue faster" is answered by fixing the Meridian economics (repricing, scoping support, or accepting the concentration consciously) before adding more top-line.
- Keeps recurring revenue, one-off revenue, pipeline, and cash as separate numbers if any of those come up — does not present $18k as a single clean "revenue" figure without qualifying it as MRR specifically.
