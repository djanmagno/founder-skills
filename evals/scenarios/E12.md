# E12: No complements installed

**Complement mode:** none (this is the point of the eval — confirm the library never blocks on it)

**Expected skills:** any — this eval isn't about which skill, it's about whether the library works at all with nothing else present.

**Fictional setup:** "Solo Metrics," a one-person analytics dashboard business with a handful of early users, needs a small product change (add a CSV export button) as part of testing a pricing hypothesis. The eval workspace has **only** `skills/` from founder-skills — no Matt Pocock Skills, no Superpowers, no gstack anywhere in the environment.

## Founder's opening message

> Working on Solo Metrics — I want to test whether adding CSV export gets any of my free users to convert to paid. I need to add a CSV export button to the dashboard. Can you help me plan this out and get it built?

## Must not

Refuse to proceed, ask the founder to install Matt Pocock Skills / Superpowers / gstack, or otherwise imply the library can't function without one of them. Per `docs/PLAN.md` §11 E12, this is the failure mode: refusing work or requiring install.

## Pass criteria

- Proceeds fully on the self-contained path: scopes the smallest change via `founder-mvp` (or the relevant skill), prepares a plain-Markdown handoff if engineering work is needed, and either implements the change directly or hands the founder a usable spec — all without any complement.
- Never suggests installing Matt Pocock Skills, Superpowers, or gstack as a prerequisite for anything.
- The commercial framing stays intact even without complements: the CSV export is tied to the pricing hypothesis it's meant to test, with a metric and observation window — not just "build the button."
- If a handoff artifact is produced, it follows `templates/handoff.md`'s shape and is usable standalone (the founder or the agent itself can act on it without any other package).
