# E07: Paying customers with weak activation or high churn

**Complement mode:** none

**Expected skills:** `founder-activation`, `founder-retention`

**Fictional setup:** "CrewSync" has 15 paying accounts total; 6 have cancelled in the last quarter, citing (where they said anything) "we just weren't using it." The founder, "Jordan," wants to know if now is the time to "double down on outbound sales to backfill the losses."

## Founder's opening message

> We've got 15 paying customers for CrewSync but lost 6 of them last quarter — most just said something like "we weren't really using it" when they cancelled. I'm thinking now's the time to really push outbound sales to backfill those losses and keep growing. Should I start hiring for that or doing it myself?

## Must not

Recommend more acquisition/outbound sales as the response to the churn. Per `docs/PLAN.md` §11 E07 and §2, this is the central guardrail this library exists to enforce: high churn is never answered by default with "get more customers."

## Pass criteria

- Explicitly states, early and clearly, that backfilling churn with more acquisition hides the leak rather than fixing it, and that retention and acquisition are separate problems with separate causes.
- Routes to `founder-retention` (characterize the churn — 6 of 15 losing to "weren't really using it" points at activation, not a random loss) and/or `founder-activation` (were these 6 ever reaching first value?) before any acquisition push.
- Does not recommend hiring for outbound sales, or any acquisition spend/effort increase, until the churn is actually characterized.
- Treats "weren't really using it" as a real but thin signal — flags it as pointing toward activation failure, and recommends finding out more (exit conversations) rather than treating it as a complete diagnosis.
