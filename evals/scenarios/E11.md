# E11: Need to reposition, pivot, or kill a hypothesis

**Complement mode:** none

**Expected skills:** `founder-pivot` → `founder-review`

**Fictional setup:** "Quotient" (pricing-intelligence tool for e-commerce sellers) has run three consecutive validation experiments over 4 months, all concluded, all showing the same result: stated interest is easy to get, but nobody will commit to a paid pilot. The founder, "Lin," is ready to change direction.

Seed the eval workspace's `.founder/experiments.md` with this pre-existing content before the run starts:

```markdown
## Cold outreach pilot offer — 2026-04-02

**Status:** concluded

**Hypothesis:** e-commerce sellers doing $50k-500k/mo revenue will commit to a paid pilot ($200/mo) for automated competitor price tracking.

**Action:** 40 cold outreach messages to sellers matching the profile, offering a paid pilot.

**Owner:** Lin

**Cost:** ~15 hours.

**Metric:** number of signed pilot commitments.

**Observation window:** 3 weeks (2026-04-02 to 2026-04-23).

**Evidence:** 11 replies expressing interest (stated intent), 0 pilot commitments.

**Decision:** hypothesis not supported at this price/channel. Try a lower-friction offer next.

## Free-tier trial offer — 2026-05-05

**Status:** concluded

**Hypothesis:** removing price friction (free 14-day trial, no card) converts stated interest into actual usage commitment.

**Action:** offered the same 11 interested sellers a free trial, plus 20 new cold contacts.

**Owner:** Lin

**Cost:** ~10 hours plus trial infrastructure time.

**Metric:** number of sellers who complete trial setup.

**Observation window:** 3 weeks (2026-05-05 to 2026-05-26).

**Evidence:** 6 of 31 started setup, 1 completed it, 0 said they'd pay after.

**Decision:** hypothesis not supported — friction wasn't the blocker. Investigate whether the problem itself is not painful enough to pay for.
```

## Founder's opening message

> I've run two validation experiments over the past couple months for Quotient (automated price tracking for e-commerce sellers) and neither worked — people say they're interested but won't commit to paying, even when I removed the friction with a free trial. I'm ready to consider pivoting. What should I do?

## Must not

Erase, silently rewrite, or discard the prior experiment entries when recording the pivot decision. Per `docs/PLAN.md` §11 E11 and §2, pivots keep history and mark superseded decisions — they never delete it.

## Pass criteria

- Reads and references the two existing experiment entries rather than starting a diagnosis from scratch or ignoring them.
- Routes to `founder-pivot` to compare persist / reposition / change segment-or-product / shut down, using the two experiments' evidence (repeated stated-intent-without-commitment) as the actual basis for the comparison — not vibes.
- If a new direction is chosen, the two prior entries remain in `.founder/experiments.md` **unmodified**, and a new entry (or an explicit marker) is added noting the pivot and what evidence drove it. Nothing is deleted or rewritten in place.
- Routes to `founder-review` to close out the old direction's experiment thread properly rather than leaving it in limbo.
